About this item
  • Build or Upgrade Your Arcade Stick – The Arcade Stick Agent is the controller board inside a fightstick, leverless controller or cabinet panel. Wire your buttons and lever to it, plug in USB, and it speaks to your PC or console for you. Board only.
  • Works on PC and Consoles – XInput on PC and Raspberry Pi, no drivers; native PS3, Switch and Switch 2 modes; keyboard mode for MAME. PS4, Xbox One / Series X|S and PS5 games that support arcade sticks need an auth dongle, sold separately.
  • Ready When It Arrives – Pre-flashed with GP2040-CE v0.7.12 and individually checked by SINTRON before shipping. Industry-standard 96.3 x 45.31 mm footprint with 4 mounting holes, for cases built to that size. Hardware support from SINTRON.
  • No Soldering at the Board – Strip each wire, push it into a screw terminal, tighten. Works with Japanese-style and American-style arcade buttons. A standard 20-pin harness plugs straight in. L3, R3 and A2 have their own screw terminals and a 4-pin plug.
  • Sub-1 ms Average Input Latency – 0.76 ms average in XInput mode and 0.91 ms in PS5 mode at 1000 Hz USB polling, as published by the open-source firmware project this board runs. For leverless and competitive play, where every frame counts.
  • Set Up in Your Browser, No App – Plug in, open the setup page and press each button when asked. Turbo, SOCD cleaning, macros, 6 profiles and per-button RGB (LEDs not included) are set there; the lever can act as D-pad, left or right stick.
  • One Board per Player – For a two-player cabinet, use two boards: each shows up as its own controller with its own button map. For MAME in keyboard mode, give each board different keys on the setup page. This listing includes one board.
